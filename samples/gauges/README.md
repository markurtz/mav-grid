# Gauge samples

Gauges G-1 to G-4 of section 9 of the spec: the opening blade, the depth step, the slide gauge and the setting bar. Their limits come from the limits table of `spec/data/params.json`. Gauges G-5 and G-6 are not designed yet.

A printed gauge is itself only as good as the printer. Measure it before trusting it (rule F-7).
